PROFILE 01 KGOSI D. MOGATLE
WHO I AM PROFILE / 001

A mind driven by
curiosity and possibility.

My name is Kgosi David Mogatle. I am an undergraduate student pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Business Intelligence and Data Analytics at Botswana School of Business Sciences.

I consider myself diligent, analytical and articulate. I am interested in understanding how information, technology and business knowledge work together, particularly in environments where data influences decisions, strategy and organisational performance.

My academic interests extend into computer technology and its practical application in business, as well as business finance and the principles that influence financial decisions. I am curious about how these different areas connect and how a deeper understanding of them can help me approach problems more thoughtfully.

Beyond my current studies, I want to continue developing my perspective, strengthening my capabilities and discovering how I can apply what I learn to real-world situations.
