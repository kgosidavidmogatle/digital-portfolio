My name is Kgosi David Mogatle. I am an undergraduate student pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Business Intelligence and Data Analytics at Botswana School of Business Sciences.

I consider myself diligent, analytical and articulate. I am interested in understanding how information, technology and business knowledge work together, particularly in an environment where decisions are influenced by data.

My academic interests extend into computer technology and its practical application in business, as well as business finance and the fundamental principles that guide financial decisions.
