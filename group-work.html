01 / THE EXPERIENCE

BEYOND INDIVIDUAL WORK

Different perspectives.
Shared responsibility.

Group work introduced an important dimension to my academic experience: the ability to work with other people towards an outcome that no individual member could achieve entirely alone.

It required more than completing assigned tasks. It involved listening to different perspectives, exchanging ideas, managing responsibilities and recognising that the quality of a collective result depends on the participation of its members.

02 / TEAM DEVELOPMENT

HOW A TEAM
TAKES SHAPE.

Effective collaboration is a process. The stages below provide a framework for understanding how teams develop, navigate differences and work towards shared goals.

01
STAGE ONE

Forming

Every collaborative effort begins with establishing a common understanding. Members get acquainted, consider the task and begin identifying what needs to be accomplished.

At this stage, I recognise the importance of understanding the assignment, respecting different viewpoints and establishing a shared sense of direction before rushing into execution.

FOUNDATION
02
STAGE TWO

Storming

Different personalities, opinions and approaches can create friction. Members may disagree about priorities, methods or how responsibilities should be handled.

This stage highlights the importance of patience, constructive communication and being willing to consider perspectives beyond one's own.

ADJUSTMENT
03
STAGE THREE

Norming

As the team develops, members begin to understand their responsibilities and establish more effective ways of working.

Clear expectations, mutual respect and accountability become essential. I see this stage as a reminder that successful teamwork requires every member to understand how their contribution fits into the larger objective.

ALIGNMENT
04
STAGE FOUR

Performing

A team becomes more effective when members work with a shared understanding, communicate consistently and take responsibility for their respective tasks.

The focus shifts from simply organising the work to delivering a result. This demonstrates how preparation and cooperation can translate into meaningful collective progress.

EXECUTION
05
STAGE FIVE

Adjourning

Completing a group assignment provides an opportunity to evaluate the process and acknowledge what the team has achieved.

Reflection matters because the conclusion of a task should also be an opportunity to identify what worked, what could have been better and what each member can carry into future collaborations.

REFLECTION

03 / INDIVIDUAL PERSPECTIVE

MY PLACE
IN THE TEAM.

I view my contribution to group work as more than the completion of an individual responsibility. It also involves remaining engaged with the collective objective, contributing ideas and being receptive to the input of others.

My analytical interests encourage me to approach tasks thoughtfully, while being articulate helps me recognise the value of communicating ideas clearly. I also understand that effective participation requires listening, flexibility and accountability.

Ultimately, individual contribution becomes meaningful when it supports the progress of the entire team.

05 / PERSONAL REFLECTION

THE RESULT IS
IMPORTANT.
THE PROCESS
IS EQUALLY SO.

Reflecting on teamwork has helped me appreciate that collaboration is not automatically effective simply because people are placed in a group. It develops through communication, shared responsibility and a willingness to work through differences.

In future group assignments, I would place even greater emphasis on establishing clear expectations early, maintaining consistent communication and evaluating progress throughout the process. I would also be more intentional about ensuring that every contribution remains connected to the group's overall objectives.

The greatest lesson is that working together requires both individual responsibility and collective commitment. Those are qualities I intend to continue developing throughout my academic and professional journey.
