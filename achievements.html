03 / WHAT IT REPRESENTS

Beyond the
position achieved.

Being selected to represent my school was meaningful because it gave me the opportunity to take part in a technology-focused competition beyond the classroom. Achieving third place in both 2020 and 2021 made the experience an important part of my academic history.

Looking back, I recognise that achievements are not valuable only because of the result. They also represent the experiences, preparation and exposure that contribute to personal development. Participating in the hackathon gave me an early opportunity to engage with programming and coding as practical activities rather than purely theoretical subjects.

This experience also connects with my present studies in Business Intelligence and Data Analytics. Although programming and business analytics involve different applications, both require logical thinking, structured approaches to problems and an interest in how technology can be used to create solutions.

I view these achievements as part of a continuing journey rather than a final destination. They remind me to remain curious, seek opportunities to apply what I learn and approach future challenges with determination and a willingness to improve.
