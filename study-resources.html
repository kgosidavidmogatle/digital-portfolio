01 / MY APPROACH

Study with
a purpose.

As a student in Business Intelligence and Data Analytics, I recognise that different subjects demand different approaches. Some concepts require careful reading and recall, while others become clearer through calculations, coding, interpretation and practical application.

My approach to learning is centred on understanding why something works, not merely memorising what it does. This is especially important in analytical fields, where knowledge must be applied to questions, evidence and real-world problems.

The strategies and resources on this page provide a framework for building more organised, active and independent learning habits.

02 / STUDY STRATEGIES

WHAT HELPS ME
LEARN BETTER.

01

Break topics down

Divide complex topics into smaller concepts. Understanding the foundations first makes advanced material easier to approach and reduces the temptation to memorise without understanding.

02

Practise actively

Work through questions, calculations, coding exercises and practical examples instead of relying entirely on reading. Applying an idea reveals whether I can actually use it.

03

Test my understanding

Close my notes and explain a concept in my own words. Practice questions and self-testing help identify knowledge gaps before an assessment exposes them.

04

Review consistently

Revisit material across several study sessions instead of leaving revision until the last minute. Spacing out revision supports longer-term retention and makes difficult concepts easier to recognise.

05

Connect theory to practice

Relate academic concepts to familiar situations, business problems and practical examples. Understanding where knowledge is useful can make abstract ideas more meaningful.

06

Plan with intention

Set realistic goals for each study session. Prioritise difficult topics, break assignments into manageable tasks and leave time for revision, rest and unexpected challenges.

03 / A SIMPLE WORKFLOW

FROM NEW TOPIC
TO UNDERSTANDING.

01

Explore

Preview the topic, identify its learning objectives and note the main questions I should be able to answer.

02

Understand

Study the core concepts, clarify unfamiliar terms and connect new information to previously learned material.

03

Apply

Complete exercises, solve problems, interpret examples or build a small practical task using the knowledge.

04

Evaluate

Test my understanding without relying on notes. Identify mistakes and return to the areas that need further work.

05

Revisit

Review the material after a gap, practise again and reflect on how confidently I can explain or apply the concept.

05 / PUT IT INTO PRACTICE

A MORE
INTENTIONAL STUDY SESSION.

A simple structure can make a study session easier to begin and easier to evaluate.

15

Prepare and review

Identify one clear goal, organise materials and briefly recall what I already know about the topic.

30

Learn the concept

Focus on understanding the lesson, taking concise notes and resolving unfamiliar ideas.

30

Practise independently

Work through questions or practical exercises without immediately consulting worked solutions.

15

Reflect and plan

Summarise what I learned, record unresolved questions and choose the next topic to revisit.

Suggested duration: 90 minutes. Adjust the session to suit the topic, workload, concentration level and need for breaks.

06 / A CONTINUOUS PROCESS

THERE IS ALWAYS
MORE TO LEARN.

I believe effective learning requires consistency, curiosity and the willingness to question my own understanding. It is possible to spend hours studying and still struggle to apply a concept if the process relies only on passive reading or memorisation.

A more meaningful approach involves identifying what I know, recognising what I do not yet understand, and taking deliberate steps to close that gap. Mistakes then become information about where improvement is needed rather than reasons to stop trying.

As I continue my academic journey, I want to strengthen not only what I learn but also how I learn. Developing that ability will matter beyond examinations: it will support independent thinking, professional adaptability and continued growth in a field shaped by changing technology and new analytical challenges.
