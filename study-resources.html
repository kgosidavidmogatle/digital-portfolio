01 / MY APPROACH

Study with
a purpose.

As a student in Business Intelligence and Data Analytics, I understand that different subjects require different ways of learning. Some concepts require memorisation, while others are better understood through practice and application.

The strategies below focus on making study sessions more active, organised and meaningful.

02 / STUDY STRATEGIES

WHAT HELPS ME
LEARN BETTER.

01

Break topics down

Large topics can feel overwhelming. Breaking them into smaller concepts makes it easier to focus on understanding one idea at a time.

02

Learn by practising

For technical and analytical subjects, reading alone is not always enough. Working through examples and questions helps strengthen understanding.

03

Test my understanding

Asking questions without immediately looking at notes helps identify what I understand and which areas need more attention.

04

Review consistently

Revisiting material over time helps keep important concepts familiar instead of leaving all revision until the last minute.

05

Use examples

Connecting theory to practical examples makes abstract concepts easier to understand and remember.

06

Plan the work

Setting clear study priorities helps me manage my time and give proper attention to different modules and assignments.

03 / A SIMPLE WORKFLOW

FROM NEW TOPIC
TO UNDERSTANDING.

01

Read

Start by identifying the main concepts, definitions and purpose of the topic.

02

Understand

Break difficult ideas into smaller parts and connect them to what I already know.

03

Practise

Apply the concept through examples, exercises, calculations or practical tasks.

04

Review

Return to the topic later and test whether the information can still be recalled and applied independently.
