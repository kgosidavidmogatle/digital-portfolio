PROFESSIONAL COMMUNICATION / 10

MAKE THE
FIRST IMPRESSION.

A professional email is often the first point of contact with a potential organisation. Clear communication can establish credibility before the attachments are even opened.

KDM.

01 / WHY IT MATTERS

Short message.
Professional impact.

When applying for industrial attachment, the email accompanying an application letter and curriculum vitae should be concise, respectful and purposeful.

Its role is to introduce the applicant, communicate the reason for writing and direct the recipient to the attached documents.

03 / BEFORE YOU SEND

Small details.
Big difference.

01

Use a clear subject line

Make the purpose of the email immediately understandable to the recipient.

02

Keep it concise

Avoid repeating the entire application letter in the email. Introduce yourself and explain why you are writing.

03

Check your attachments

Ensure both your CV and application letter are attached, correctly named and saved in PDF format.

04

Proofread before sending

Check spelling, grammar, recipient details and the accuracy of your contact information.
