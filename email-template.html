01 / WHY IT MATTERS

Short message.
Professional impact.

When applying for industrial attachment, the email accompanying a curriculum vitae and application letter should be concise, respectful and purposeful. Its goal is to introduce the applicant and make the reason for writing immediately clear.

Effective professional communication also demonstrates attention to detail. A clear subject line, an appropriate greeting, accurate contact information and correctly named attachments help the recipient understand and process an application.

The template below can be adapted to suit different organisations, departments and attachment opportunities.

03 / BEFORE YOU SEND

Small details.
Big difference.

01

Write a clear subject line

State the purpose of the email and include your name where appropriate. Avoid vague subjects such as "Hello" or "Request".

02

Keep the message concise

Introduce yourself, explain why you are writing and mention your attachments. Leave the detailed account of your qualifications and motivation to your application letter and CV.

03

Address the recipient appropriately

Use the recipient's name and correct title when known. If the contact person is unknown, a respectful greeting such as "Dear Hiring Manager" is appropriate.

04

Check your attachments

Confirm that your CV and application letter are attached, readable, correctly named and saved in the requested format.

05

Proofread before sending

Check spelling, grammar, recipient details and contact information. Send a test email to yourself if you want to check the layout and attachments first.

06

Follow up professionally

If you do not receive a response, allow a reasonable amount of time before sending a brief, polite follow-up. Respect any instructions or deadlines given by the organisation.
